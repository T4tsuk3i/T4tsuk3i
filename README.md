# 👾 Hey, I'm T4tsuk3i

*Taking fun things apart since forever... and putting them back together better. Loving every second of it.* 🛠️

I like figuring out how things work, breaking them apart, building things from scratch, and chasing whatever interesting problem happens to land in front of me.

Some days that's reverse engineering. Some days it's writing code. Some days it's experimenting with LLMs, messing with Linux, or going far too deep into something that was supposed to take ten minutes.

And when I'm not doing any of that, there's a good chance I'm watching **Tadej Pogačar** race. 🚴‍♂️

## 🧪 What's Brewing?

### 🌅 Project Sunrise

A community-driven effort to preserve and explore an old build of *Destiny 2* through reverse engineering.

Load into destinations, recreate missions, explore the world, experiment with game systems, and learn how the engine works along the way.

**[Project Sunrise →](https://github.com/T4tsuk3i/Sunrise-T4tsuk3i)**

### 🧠 AI Experiments

Building and experimenting with AI systems, local LLMs, assistants, memory, and everything surrounding them.

### 🧰 Reverse Engineering

Taking apart software, studying how it works, and figuring out the pieces that make it tick.

### 🐧 Linux & Systems

Experimenting with Linux, system configuration, self-hosting, development environments, and generally finding increasingly interesting ways to break my own computer.
